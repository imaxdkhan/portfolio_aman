About

I'm a final-year Computer Science undergraduate who likes the unglamorous middle of software — the part where raw, messy data becomes something structured and dependable. Most of what I build sits at that seam: pipelines that take unstructured input and turn it into schema-compliant output, and full-stack apps that hold relational and document data side by side without either one fighting the other.

Recently that's meant building a document-generation pipeline used in a live production workflow, and architecting a rental platform where PostgreSQL and MongoDB share the same backend on purpose. I'm comfortable moving between Python and the JavaScript/TypeScript stack, and I'm currently pointed toward data engineering, pipeline automation, and cloud-native systems as the next place to go deep.
